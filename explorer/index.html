How It Works
Each cell lives or dies based on its 8 neighbors:
Birth: dead cell with B neighbors → alive
Survive: live cell with S neighbors → stays
Otherwise: cell dies or stays dead
🟩 Green flash = cell was born
🔴 Red flash = cell died
